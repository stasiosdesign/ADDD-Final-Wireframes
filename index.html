What actually costs you time

Technology problems rarely exist in isolation. ADDD looks across the practice to understand what is connected, what is causing friction, and what should change first.

The ADDD approach

Fix the system, not the symptoms

Understand

Map how work, tools, information and decisions actually move through the practice, not how they are supposed to.

(01)

Diagnose

Trace the causes across workflows, BIM, software, infrastructure, data, costs, roles and AI, rather than treating each issue separately.

(02)

Prioritise

Turn what we find into clear priorities: what should change first, who should own it and where investment will have the most impact.

(03)

From a focused workflow problem to practice-wide strategy, ongoing advice or software costs. Choose the route that fits the issue.

Fit

For practices where technology is now a leadership issue.

Not primarily for
