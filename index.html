How we work

Technology decisions only make sense when you can see how work, information, systems and responsibility connect.

1

Understand

Map how work, tools, information and decisions actually operate today—not how they are assumed to operate.

2

Diagnose

Identify the connected causes across workflows, BIM, software, infrastructure, data, costs, roles and AI.

3

Prioritise

Decide what should change, in what order, who should own it and what level of investment it deserves.

Support

Stay with the decisions as they meet reality—regular guidance while priorities shift, tools change and the plan needs holding to.

Fit

For practices where technology is now a leadership issue.

ADDD is most useful when the problem is no longer isolated to one tool, one project or one team.

Best suited to

  • Established architecture practices, usually around 25-200 people.

  • Practices facing connected questions around workflows, software, costs, data, AI or ownership.

  • Firms ready to act, but unclear about what should happen first.

Not primarily for

  • Firms seeking implementation without participating in strategic decisions.

  • One-off software training, Tactical BIM production support. orOutsourced IT management.

  • Quick recommendations for a particular product.
