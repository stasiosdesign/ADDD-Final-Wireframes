Does This Sound Like Your Practice?

Most practices don’t have a software problem. They have a workflow problem. The same issues recur across projects. Teams solve similar problems in different ways. Information gets lost or recreated. Tools accumulate without clear purpose.

The same problems surface on every project

Coordination delays, rework and information loss repeat without lasting solutions

Teams work in different ways across the practice

No shared method means knowledge stays siloed and effort duplicates

Information disappears between handoffs

Data is recreated rather than passed forward, wasting time and introducing error

Growth has exposed weak foundations

Systems that worked at 30 people don’t scale to 80 without breaking

Common Questions

  • Who should attend the workshop?

    Anyone who shapes how work moves through the practice — a director or partner, the project lead for the chosen project, and whoever owns BIM, IT or operations.

  • How many people should come?

    Between four and eight. Enough perspectives to see the whole flow, few enough that everyone contributes.

  • What preparation is required?

    A short questionnaire and the selection of one real project. Nothing else needs to be produced in advance.

  • Does it have to be in person?

    Yes. The workshop works because people are in the room together, working through a real project. Remote doesn’t create the same clarity.

  • When do we get the report?

    Within two weeks of the session.
